The process it installs

Nine rooms. One hallway.

Every brief walks the same hallway, and every room leaves a note for the next. Scroll and follow it.

01 · Intake · sorter

The board, before coffee.

Briefs come in from Asana or ClickUp. They're sorted by their status field, not the column they sit in, and anything missing is asked for before Figma opens.

readsbriefs, due dates, attachmentswritesreadiness per brief, client questions
02 · Brand · librarian

A website in. A brand out.

brand-extract reads the brand's site with dembrandt: colours, type, components, motion. rules-sweep reads 30 days of comments and client edits and writes what was said twice as a rule.

readsbrand URL, comments, client-edited frameswritesBRAND.md, The Rules File (hard and soft)
03 · Library · librarian

The law is five to ten emails.

The approved emails become a library: row sequence, hero signature, module family, product capacity, last used. A few templates set the rules for everything after.

readsapproved emails in Figmawriteslibrary.json
04 · Layers

Name it, or it doesn't exist.

Email Layers gives every row a name and an order. The audit flags anything an agent couldn't read. Group 234 doesn't make it through.

readsthe Figma file, through Figma MCPwritesa layer report, fixes only on your yes
05 · Atomic · librarian

Cut once. Reuse forever.

Approved emails are split into organisms, molecules and atoms. A reviewer reads the dry run before a single part is written.

readsan approved emailwritesnamed parts, ready for the next build
06 · Match + Remix · sorter, maker

The right precedent, with reasons.

The copy is matched to approved templates slot by slot. For a sale, last year's matching send. For the rest, a hero and a module unused in the last 8 sends. What the brief fixes stays locked.

readsbrief copy, library.jsonwritesa precedent and a copy-to-row map
07 · Build · maker

No new elements.

The maker duplicates approved rows and reorders, resizes, flips and swaps copy and photos in the client's Figma file. Emailify-ready. Links stay a manual step.

readsthe precedent rowswritesthe email, in Figma
08 · Review · reviewer

Evidence, then a verdict.

Preflight runs the 7 point checklist and gives one of four verdicts: Ready, Ready with substitutions, Not ready, Unverified. Model Review checks the checker against a human.

readsthe built email, the ruleswritesa decision card and a review deck
You

Only you say yes.

Accept or reject each finding. A rejection that repeats goes back to the rules, so next week the studio already knows.

readsthe deck, on your phone or in Figmawritesapproved, or changes
09 · Run · studio lead

The week, on rails.

The lead plans the week, runs it in date order, and reports where the time went with a source on every number. The capacity calculator gives one honest number for the busy months.

readsthe week plan, your task exportwritesthe week report, your capacity number

FAQ

Fair questions.

Is this done for you?

No. You install it and run it yourself, on your machine and your files. If you want it fitted to your studio, book a 1:1 setup call.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (Claude Code for the plugin, or the Claude app for the skill zips), Figma, and Asana or ClickUp for briefs. Emailify for export. Brand extraction needs Node 18 or newer.

Does it design new emails from scratch?

No, on purpose. It rebuilds every email from rows the client already approved, and flags anything that would need a new element.

Will it touch my client's files without asking?

Never. Every skill drafts, then stops. A human approves before anything changes in a file or a rulebook.

Is the workshop live or recorded?

Recorded. Recording date: to confirm.
