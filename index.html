The rules we work by

Taste does not scale. Standards do.

Every skill in the studio is one of these rules, written so a model can follow it the day it joins. Pinned here the way they hang in the studio.

Rule 01Build

No new elements.

Every email is rebuilt from rows the client already approved. Duplicate, reorder, resize, flip, swap copy and photos. Anything new is flagged, never invented.

Rule 02Brand

Feedback that happens twice is a rule you forgot to write.

Rule 03Layers

Agents can't read Group 234.

Email Layers gives every row a name and an order, so a model can find it, reuse it and check it.

Rule 04Review

AI drafts. Humans approve.

No skill edits a file, a rulebook or a client's Figma without an explicit yes.

Rule 05Library
5 to 10

Templates first.

Pick the five to ten approved templates that become the law for a brand. Everything new follows them.

Rule 06Atomic

Cut it once. Reuse it forever.

Atomic Emails splits approved emails into organisms, molecules and atoms, after a reviewer reads the dry run.

Rule 07Run

Capacity = Systems × Standards.

One honest number for what your studio can ship, counted from your own task export.

Rule 08Match + Remix
90/10

The model drafts the 90.

Layouts, variations and drafts come ready. The designer gets a short list, and the final say.

The process it installs

Nine stages, one plugin.

Each stage is a skill, or a small team: a lead plans, a maker drafts, a reviewer checks with evidence, and you approve. Scroll the board.

01 · Intake

The board, sorted.

Reads briefs from Asana or ClickUp, sorts them by their status field (not the column), and flags missing fields, date conflicts and AI images before anyone opens Figma.

The brand, extracted.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is this done for you?

No. You install it and run it yourself, on your own machine and your own files. If you want it fitted to your studio, book a 1:1 setup call.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan (Claude Code for the full plugin, or the Claude app for the skill zips), Figma, and Asana or ClickUp for briefs. Emailify for export. Brand extraction needs Node 18 or newer.

Does it design new emails from scratch?

No, on purpose. It rebuilds every email from rows the client already approved, and flags anything that would need a new element.

Will it touch my client's files without asking?

Never. Every skill drafts, then stops. A human approves before anything is applied to a file or a rulebook.

Is the workshop live or recorded?
