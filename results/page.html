01 · The problem

Volume broke the old way.

Emaily has designed emails since July 2021. This year that meant 2,705 designs for 102 brands, about 15 every business day, and 542 in July alone. People were not the slow part. These seven things were.

Briefs live in two tools

Asana for some clients, ClickUp for others, and the board column rarely says where a brief really stands.

Fixed by Any brief, fitted

Layouts start repeating

The same hero three weeks running reads as lazy, even when each email on its own is fine.

Fixed by Plan the week

Rules buried in comments

"Use the brand font only in headlines" lives in one comment from one Thursday. Next month nobody remembers it.

Fixed by Learn a brand

Wrong product or colour

A model in navy on a charcoal link. A photo left over from the template. Small, and the client always sees it.

Fixed by Preflight

Copy drifts from the brief

Designers fill gaps with their own headlines. One altered line means one more round with the client.

Fixed by Copy Bouncer

Review is a maze

Pages per run, frames everywhere, and nothing says what changed since the last version or why.

Fixed by The dossier

House rules

What the studio never does.

  • Every word comes from the brief or the product page
  • The website wins on names, prices and photos
  • Builds only from the brand's approved emails and their parts
  • Never touches the footer, never builds in a client's file
  • Never posts, sends, schedules or touches your ESP
  • Never reads your passwords or tokens
Always a person

Before export, you check.

  • Links set in your email plugin
  • Mobile stacking and dark mode
  • Gmail clipping and alt text
  • AI images OK'd by the client
  • Export as HTML or Klaviyo blocks from Emailify
